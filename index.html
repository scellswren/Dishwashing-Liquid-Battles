Instructions

Goal: knock your opponent's health to 0, or knock them clean out of the arena.

  • 🕹️Space/W/↑ jump   A/← left   D/→ right
  • ✨E main ability   Q secondary ability   R special ability
  • 👆On touch devices: use the on-screen arrows, or drag anywhere on the arena to steer your fighter. Tap the E/Q/R buttons for abilities.
  • 🎮Controller support: Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo controllers all work automatically — just plug in (or connect via Bluetooth) and press any button. Move with the left stick, jump with the bottom face button (A / ✕ / B), and the ability bar will relabel itself to show the exact face button for each ability on your controller.
  • ⏱️Every ability has a cooldown shown right on its button — watch the timer count down.
  • 🧱The arena is fully walled in — you cannot fall out. Knock your opponent's health to zero to win.
  • 🗺️Pick a map — City, Wasteland, or Battleworld — from the mode-select screen before you fight. Watch out for the glowing green toxic waste puddle on Wasteland; standing on it will slowly damage you.
  • 🪙Modes & tokens: Practice lets you pick Random, Easy, Medium or Hard opponents. Battle gives you and your rival 3 lives each and pays 10 tokens for a win. Spend 100 tokens on Unlock Character to challenge a random fighter — win to unlock them, or get your tokens back if you already own them.
  • 🎒Your owned and locked fighters live together in Inventory. Select a fighter there to equip them, then hit Play from the main menu to battle with them against a random opponent. Your roster is saved in this browser.
